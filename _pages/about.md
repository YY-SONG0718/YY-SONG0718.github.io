I am Dr Yuyao Song, and I am a computational biologist specializing in **multi-omics** and **machine learning in biomedicine**. I enjoy abstracting biological problems and build ML models, softwares and pipelines to generate decision-leading insights. I am currently working as a Bioinformatics scientist at [Insmed Innnovation UK](https://insmed.com/) based in Cambridge, UK. Previously, I completed my PhD at [EMBL-EBI](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/people/person/yuyao-song/) working with [Prof Irene Papatheodorou](https://www.earlham.ac.uk/profile/irene-papatheodorou) and [Prof Detlev Arendt](https://www.embl.org/groups/arendt/). My PhD research lies in **cross-species comparison of cell types using single cell transcriptomics data**. I have developed computational frameworks to tackle the conservation and divergence of cell type expression profiles across species, from the perspective of evolution and functional genomics. I am an expert in scRNA-seq analysis, and leveraging from basic machine learning to latest foundation models and sequence-to-function models to perform advanced multi-omics analysis.

I spend most of my free time exploring nature and culture across the globe. It is my life goal to visit as many places on earth as possible. I enjoy going to science and art museums, reading true history books and dancing. I am particularly enthusiastic about experiencing concerts and opera in different countries. I grew up by the sea and am deeply in love with the sea, more so at night.
