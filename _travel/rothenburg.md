Rothenburg ob der Tauber sits on a ridge above the Tauber valley in northern Bavaria. The
town wall still runs the whole way round, and you can walk most of it under cover. Inside,
the streets kept their late medieval shape because the town was too poor to rebuild after
the Thirty Years' War.
