The Dolomites are a block of pale limestone in the north of Italy, between Trentino, South
Tyrol and Belluno. In April the valleys are green and the peaks still hold snow. We drove in
through Cadore, walked around Lago di Braies, and spent the last day above Val di Funes
looking at the Odle group from the meadows on the far side of the valley.
