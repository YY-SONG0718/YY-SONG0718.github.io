Shanxi lies west of the Taihang mountains, and its name means exactly that. The province
holds more old timber buildings than any other part of China. We started in Taiyuan, the
capital, with the provincial museum and the Jinci temple, then took the train north to
Datong for the Yungang grottoes and the Liao dynasty temples in the old town.
