I've never seen the sea so blue 💙💙💙, its as blue as a neon highlighter pen! Zakynthos is the southernmost of the large Ionian islands. The west coast is a wall of white
limestone, and the only way to see most of it is from the water. We took a boat from the
north of the island to Navagio, the shipwreck beach, and then to the Blue Caves at Cape
Skinari. The wreck on the beach is the Panagiotis, a coaster that ran aground in 1980.
