Name Yuyao Song
Label Computational Biologist
Email sylvie.yuyao.song@gmail.com

Summary Computational biologist with expertise in single cell transcriptomics, multi-omics and machine learning, applied to functional genomics and drug development. First author of three methodological publications on cross-species single cell analysis in Nature Communications, Bioinformatics and Genome Biology, and co-author on consortium papers in Nature and Nature Biotechnology. I have developed several open-source tools for the analysis of single cell RNA-seq data that the research community has adopted. I now apply this expertise to therapeutic target discovery and drug development at an international pharmaceutical company.
